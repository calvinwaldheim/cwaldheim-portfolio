## Smart Asset Management Platform — ERIKS Digital

When I took over ERIKS’ IoT product, it was a standalone platform tracking vibration data from industrial pumps and motors — valuable, but isolated. Across the company, similar tools existed in silos: different systems for monitoring, tagging, and maintaining assets. None spoke the same language.

I saw an opportunity to turn fragmented technologies into a single, scalable domain — a unified Smart Asset Management Platform capable of providing real-time operational insight across ERIKS’ entire installed base.

Through product vision, stakeholder alignment, and close collaboration with engineering, I became responsible for the global portfolio of asset-management solutions, shaping a multi-year roadmap that connected data, devices, and users across the enterprise.

### Key Contributions

- Unified siloed systems: Consolidated standalone monitoring and maintenance tools into the core IoT platform, creating one consistent customer experience across Europe and the UK.
- Modernized asset identification: Replaced outdated barcode systems with dynamic QR-code tagging, enabling digital traceability and lifecycle analytics for tens of thousands of assets.
- Led large-scale migration: Decommissioned two legacy hose-management systems in operation for nearly two decades, seamlessly migrating 1,000+ customers to the new secure platform.
- Launched predictive insights: Introduced a real-time pump monitoring and anomaly-detection engine, combining multi-sensor data streams into a unified analytics layer that detects performance issues early and powers new service-revenue models.

### Impact

This transformation repositioned ERIKS’ IoT platform from a single-purpose sensor hub into an enterprise-grade Asset Intelligence ecosystem.
It delivered measurable improvements in uptime, operational visibility, and scalability—reducing maintenance costs, unlocking new predictive-service revenue, and unifying global workflows under one strategic data backbone.
